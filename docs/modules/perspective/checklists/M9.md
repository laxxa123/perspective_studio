# M9 — The eye and the View: device checklist (release 0.10.0)

- [ ] Open a scene made with 0.9.0: it looks exactly as before (converted on open).
- [ ] No VP capsules: off-screen VPs show as a small arrow + tiny label at the screen edge; tap one → the view pans to that VP; in Persp., drag it → the VP moves.
- [ ] Ground point: three thin coloured lines, no arrowheads, no dashed lines to the VPs.
- [ ] Select a box: only its edges extend toward their VPs (one line per edge); no fans from the VPs in any Display preset.
- [ ] Sketch: open the options — tools, colours, four sizes and Free / Soft / Locked are all visible without scrolling; the sizes draw 1 / 2 / 4 / 6 px lines.
- [ ] Select the cube → View (rotate icon): Turn and Tilt sliders appear; the readout says "around the selected object"; the toolbar is hidden.
- [ ] Drag Turn: the cube turns in place and keeps its size. Tap L / R: that face is square on (its horizontal lines are parallel). Tap ¾: corner view.
- [ ] Drag Tilt up to Top: the horizon leaves the screen, verticals converge to VP-V at the centre; the eye height in the readout rises.
- [ ] Revert → back to the start view. Open View again, change it, Apply → one Undo restores the previous view.
- [ ] With nothing selected, View turns around the ground point.
- [ ] After Apply at Top or L / R, Persp. mini bar says "VPs at infinity …" and VP handles are hidden; move Turn / Tilt off the end → handles return.
- [ ] Persp.: drag VP-R → open View → the Turn knob shows the new angle.
- [ ] Toolbar set left in Settings → the Tilt slider sits on the left.
- [ ] A scene with strokes: opening View shows the "strokes stay on the paper" note once.
