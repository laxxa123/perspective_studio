# M2 — Canvas & perspective setup: device checklist

Release 0.4.0. Acceptance (§16): drag every control; the box responds live at
60 fps; clamping works; zoom never changes geometry.

- [ ] Opens on the paper with the horizon, the anchor (+) and the grey test cube; off-screen VPs show as coloured chips (VP-L blue, VP-R orange, VP-V purple).
- [ ] Two fingers pan and pinch-zoom (0.05×–20×); geometry does not change with zoom.
- [ ] Fit paper (rectangle icon) and Fit all (arrows icon: paper + all VPs).
- [ ] Tap a chip → the view centres on that VP.
- [ ] Perspective tool: objects dim; handles VP-L, VP-R, VP-V, Anchor appear.
- [ ] Drag VP-L and VP-R along the horizon; the cube re-projects live and smoothly.
- [ ] Drag the horizon (the grey line): VP-L and VP-R move with it.
- [ ] Drag VP-V toward the horizon: a purple band appears and VP-V stops at its edge with a vibration tick; drag well past the band and it jumps across (looking up).
- [ ] Drag the anchor: the cube moves with it; it cannot go above the horizon.
- [ ] Drag a chip in the Perspective tool → drags that VP.
- [ ] 2-point / 3-point switch: VP-V disappears / reappears below the horizon.
- [ ] Lock (padlock) disables the Perspective tool.
- [ ] A second finger during a drag cancels the drag (the system snaps back) and pans / zooms instead.
- [ ] Close and reopen the app: the view (pan / zoom) is restored. (The perspective itself resets until documents are saved in M4.)
